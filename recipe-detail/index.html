Filipino Turon

250 cal

35g carbs

6g protein

Ingredients

  • 2 ripe bananas (mashed)
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp sugar (adjust to taste)
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp nutmeg

Instructions

  1. In a bowl, mash the bananas until smooth.
  2. Add sugar, cinnamon, nutmeg, salt, and vanilla. Mix well.
  3. Stir in the flour, baking powder, and milk (or water) until a thick batter forms.
  4. Heat oil in a pan over medium heat.
  5. Drop spoonfuls of batter into the hot oil and fry until golden brown.
  6. Remove and drain on paper towels.
  7. Serve warm with powdered sugar, honey, or maple syrup.
